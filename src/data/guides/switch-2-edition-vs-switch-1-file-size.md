Upgrading a game to its Nintendo Switch 2 Edition usually makes it bigger. Higher-resolution textures, new content and new modes all take space. We compared the official eShop sizes of the Switch 1 version and the Switch 2 Edition for 34 games in the GameSpecsDB database.

## The short answer

In October 2026, of those 34 games:

- **29 grew** by more than 5%.
- **4 stayed about the same.**
- **1 shrank.**

Many of them doubled or more. On average, the Switch 2 Edition needs a bit under twice the space of the Switch 1 version.

## The biggest jumps

Among games that need more than 3 GB on Switch 2:

| Game | Switch 1 | Switch 2 Edition | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rune Factory: Guardians of Azuma | 6.1 GB | 18.1 GB | ×3.0 |
| Kirby and the Forgotten Land | 5.8 GB | 16.7 GB | ×2.9 |
| Xenoblade Chronicles 2 | 13.2 GB | 37.7 GB | ×2.9 |
| Sid Meier's Civilization VII | 8.7 GB | 22.6 GB | ×2.6 |
| Disney Dreamlight Valley | 9.9 GB | 24.6 GB | ×2.5 |
| Xenoblade Chronicles: Definitive Edition | 13.7 GB | 33.8 GB | ×2.5 |
| Super Mario Bros. Wonder | 3.7 GB | 8.9 GB | ×2.4 |
| Super Mario Party Jamboree | 5.4 GB | 12.8 GB | ×2.4 |
| Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds | 11.6 GB | 26.9 GB | ×2.3 |
| SHUTEN ORDER | 14.3 GB | 28.7 GB | ×2.0 |

In gigabytes, Xenoblade Chronicles 2 is the largest increase: 24.5 GB more, more than many complete games.

Games that change little either already had high-quality assets on Switch 1, or the Switch 2 Edition mostly improves performance rather than adding new content.

## Why some Editions grow so much

- **Higher-resolution textures.** Running at 1440p or 4K on a TV needs sharper textures, and textures are the largest part of most games.
- **New content.** Some packs add a whole new mode or story, like Jamboree TV for Super Mario Party Jamboree or Star-Crossed World for Kirby and the Forgotten Land.

## Does the price tell you anything?

Not much. Some free upgrades grow a lot (Disney Dreamlight Valley ×2.5), and some paid ones barely change (Animal Crossing, $4.99, from 10.6 to 10.8 GB). Check the size on the game page, and the price in the [list of every upgrade](/lists/switch-2-edition-upgrades/).

## What it means for your storage

If you plan to upgrade several Switch 1 games, add up the difference before you do it. Upgrading Kirby, both Xenoblade Chronicles games and Civilization VII, for example, adds around 70 GB compared with the Switch 1 versions. The [storage calculator](/tools/storage-calculator/) helps you plan, and our guide on [Switch 2 Edition, upgrade packs and free updates](/guides/switch-2-edition-upgrade-packs-explained/) explains what each upgrade adds.

## Common questions

**Do physical Switch 2 Editions save space?** Nintendo's Switch 2 Editions in our database all have the full game on the cartridge, so they only store updates and saves. Some third-party Editions are a download code in the box and need the full size.
