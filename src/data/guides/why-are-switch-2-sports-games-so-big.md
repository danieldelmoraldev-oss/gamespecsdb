If your Switch 2 storage filled up overnight, a sports game is the usual suspect. Yearly sports series are some of the largest downloads on the system, bigger than almost every Nintendo game and most open-world adventures.

## Why they are so large

- **Hundreds of real players and arenas.** Licensed sports games include detailed models, faces, kits and stadiums for whole leagues, and each of them needs high-resolution textures.
- **Commentary and crowd audio.** Play-by-play commentary is recorded in many lines for every situation, often in several languages, and audio takes a lot of space.
- **Many modes in one package.** Career modes, online team-building modes, story modes and seasonal content all ship in the same download.
- **Yearly releases built on the same base as the other consoles.** These games are developed for PlayStation, Xbox and PC at the same time, and the Switch 2 version carries much of the same content.

## None of them save space on a cartridge

Every major sports game we track is a [Game-Key Card](/guides/what-is-a-game-key-card/) or a download code in the box. In both cases the full game is downloaded to the console, so buying a boxed copy does not save any storage. If you want a sports game, you need the full size free.

## How to plan for them

1. **Keep one season at a time.** When the new edition comes out, archive the old one. NBA 2K26 and NBA 2K27 together take more than 150 GB, well over half of the internal storage.
2. **Leave room for updates.** Sports games get roster and season updates throughout the year, so do not fill the console to the last gigabyte.
3. **Consider a card if you play several series.** One football, one basketball and one wrestling game together take around 245 GB with the sizes above, which is more than the free space on a Switch 2 on its own. A [microSD Express card](/guides/switch-2-microsd-express-cards/) of 256 GB or more fixes that.
4. **Check before you buy.** Every game page shows the current size, and the [storage calculator](/tools/storage-calculator/) tells you whether it fits with the rest of your library.

## Performance trade-offs

Fitting these games on Switch 2 sometimes comes with compromises. EA SPORTS FC 26, for example, is capped at 30 FPS on Switch 2 while the other consoles run at 60 FPS. Where we have sourced performance figures for a sports game, they are on its game page.

## Common questions

**Is the size the same as on other consoles?** Not necessarily. Sizes vary by platform because of compression, texture resolution and which modes are included. Always check the Switch 2 listing, not another platform's.

**Do sports games keep growing?** They can. Season updates and new content are added over the year, so the size on each game page shows the date it was last checked.
